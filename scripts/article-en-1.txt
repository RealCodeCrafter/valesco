The Magic of Motion: 5 Hidden Heroes Saving Your Car Every Day
When we think of car power, horsepower, engine displacement, and torque immediately come to mind. But have you ever wondered if all this iron might would turn into a heap of immobile junk without five unnoticeable yet vital fluids? Choosing the right motor oil, antifreeze, and transmission fluids is not just about car maintenance; it is a guarantee of your safety and savings on repairs. Let’s break down how these "hidden heroes" work and why Valesco Oil products are becoming the number one choice for car owners.
1. Motor Oil: The Heart and Blood of Your Engine
A car engine makes thousands of revolutions per minute. Without lubrication, the colossal friction would destroy the metal in a matter of seconds. Motor oil performs three essential functions:
Protection against wear: Creates a microscopic yet ultra-strong film between moving parts.
Cooling: Dissipates heat from those engine zones that antifreeze cannot reach.
Cleaning: Washes away carbon deposits and holds microparticles in suspension, preventing them from clogging the system.
Tip from Valesco Oil: Switching from mineral oil to high-quality synthetics reduces fuel consumption by up to 3% and eases engine startup in freezing weather.
2. Transmission Fluid: Smoothness in Every Shift
No matter how powerful the engine is, a car cannot move from its spot without a gearbox. Transmission fluid operates under extreme pressure conditions. In manual transmissions, it protects gears from chipping, while in automatic transmissions (AT), it serves as the working fluid that transmits torque. High-quality transmission fluid prevents jerking when shifting gears and extends the lifespan of an expensive assembly.
3. Antifreeze (Coolant): Climate Control Under the Hood
Did you know that only about 30% of fuel combustion energy goes into moving the car? The rest is pure heat. If it is not dissipated, the engine will "boil". Antifreeze protects the cooling system from two extremes:
It does not freeze at extreme sub-zero temperatures (unlike water, which would simply crack the cylinder block).
It has an elevated boiling point and protects internal channels from corrosion and cavitation.
4. Brake Fluid: Seconds That Save Lives
This is the most critical element of safety. When you press the brake pedal, brake fluid instantly transfers this force to the brake pads. The main properties of a good brake fluid are incompressibility and a high boiling point. If the fluid is old and has absorbed moisture from the air, it can boil during sharp braking. The resulting gas bubbles compress easily, and the brake pedal simply "sinks". Regular inspection of this fluid is a law of safety.
5. Hydraulic Oil: Power and Smoothness of Control
Modern cars and specialized equipment cannot function without hydraulic systems. In passenger cars, this is most often the power steering (PAS), which allows you to turn the steering wheel literally with one finger. Hydraulic oil transfers mechanical energy throughout the system. It must maintain its viscosity in both severe heat and bitter cold so that steering remains sharp and predictable.
